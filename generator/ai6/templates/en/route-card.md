# Route Card (English)

## Four questions

1. What will you deliver? One sentence, not "learn AI video".
2. Who is on screen: a person, a product, or neither?
3. Length, ratio, platform?
4. Maximum budget and hours this week?

## Route

- [ ] A Drama: 90s, 16 shots, 2 characters, 3–5 days
- [ ] B Short: 15/30s, 1–3 shots, 2–3 hours
- [ ] C Brand/mood: 5s hero + 15s cut, 1–2 days

## Kill criteria

- Same shot, same failure ____ times: change the shot, stop retrying.
- Spend over ¥____: stop generating, cut what you have.
- ____ hours before deadline: cut to ____ seconds.
- Faces mismatch across 3 shots: go back to the character bible.

## Seven days

| Day | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One sentence + reference | You can describe "done" |
| 2 | Install one editor + one generator | You can export 5s |
| 3 | Generate 5, keep 1 | You know the failure rate |
| 4 | Voice/music/subtitles | It plays on a phone |
| 5 | Fix only the weakest shot | One shot improves |
| 6 | Export + QC | 12 checks pass |
| 7 | Publish privately + 5 lines | You know cost and next fix |
