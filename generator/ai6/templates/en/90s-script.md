# 90s Drama Script (English)

## Logline

`Someone` wants `something`, but `someone/something` blocks it, at the cost of `something`.

## Five beats (90 seconds)

| Time | Beat | Must happen | Audience knows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | Hook | One unusual action or line | Who, where, what is wrong |
| 10–30s | Want | A concrete request | What they want |
| 30–55s | Resistance | Refusal, hiding, deflection | Who blocks, why |
| 55–75s | Turn | One piece of evidence or truth | It is not what they thought |
| 75–90s | Landing | A choice or an open question | Why watch the next episode |

## Dialogue rules

- One line per shot, 6–10 English words.
- No inner monologue; write what can be said out loud.
- Every line changes the relationship: request, refusal, reveal, choice.
- Do not explain backstory; show it through action.

## 16-shot list

Use `16-shot-list.csv`. Fill the 8 key shots first, then complete.

## Sound

- Dialogue: one line per take, 48kHz, -14 LUFS.
- Ambience: room tone continuous, no cuts.
- Music: enter at 55s, do not cover the whole piece.
- Captions: 18–24 characters per line, max two lines, not over faces.

## Self-check

- [ ] Who wants what within 10s.
- [ ] Who blocks within 30s.
- [ ] A real turn at 55s, not "it was fine".
- [ ] Ends on a choice or question.
- [ ] Every shot has a purpose; no decorative filler.
