# Character Bible (English)

## C01

- Age 28–32
- Narrow face, defined cheekbones, clear jawline
- Straight eyebrows, normal eye spacing, tired eyes
- Straight nose, thin lips
- Black mid-length straight hair, low ponytail, loose strand over left ear
- Grey knit top, dark jacket, no pattern/logo
- Thin silver ring on right index finger; no earrings
- Slim build, narrow shoulders
- Restrained, alert, rarely laughs

Prompt: `C01, Chinese woman, 30 years old, oval face with defined cheekbones, straight eyebrows, tired eyes, thin lips, black mid-length straight hair in a low ponytail, grey knit top, dark jacket, thin silver ring on right index finger, slim build, restrained expression.`

Never: heavy makeup, red lip, curly hair, earrings, logo, age drift.

## C02

- Age 55–60
- Square face, deep nasolabial folds
- Thick eyebrows, downturned eyes
- Wide nose, full lips
- Grey short hair, right part
- Dark blue work shirt, rolled sleeves
- Old watch on left wrist; no ring
- Sturdy, slightly stooped
- Silent, stubborn, avoids eye contact

Prompt: `C02, Chinese man, 58 years old, square face, deep nasolabial folds, thick eyebrows, downturned eyes, wide nose, full lips, grey short hair parted on the right, dark blue work shirt with rolled sleeves, old watch on left wrist, sturdy build, slightly stooped, silent expression.`
